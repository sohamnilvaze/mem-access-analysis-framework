Description:-
1) gemm: blocked matrix multiplication , correctly identified
2) atax:- matrix vector multiplication with column major access on sparse matrix, 
3) bfs:- breadth first search on graphs, random/indirect access due to pointer-chasing, correctly identified
4) particlefilter: monte carlo localization, particles stored in unstructed arrays, correctly identified
5) srad: row-major sequential , misclassified as column major misclassified as column-major due to structural similarity between row-major and column-major access patterns under stride-based feature representation
6) cg:- sparse matrix-vector multiply, sequential within rows and jumps between, classified as block access due to localized sequential accesses within sparse rows, while also exhibiting indirect behavior due to irregular jumps across rows

Overall, the model demonstrates strong generalization to unseen workloads, accurately capturing both dominant and hybrid memory access behaviors. High-confidence predictions are observed for structured workloads (e.g., GEMM, BFS), while mixed patterns (e.g., CG) result in distributed predictions, reflecting the inherent complexity of real-world memory access patterns.
